`main.ts` writes one line (at most 180 characters, at most 25 words), a one-star review of Monday as if it were a restaurant, to `/output/entry.txt`:

> One star. Monday served cold coffee, lukewarm Wi-Fi, and a meeting that could have been an email. Will not be returning.
